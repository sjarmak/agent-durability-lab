## Product synthesis

The [coding-agent durability v1 specification](../product/coding-agent-durability-v1.md)
turns the admitted evidence into a reference-first internal proposal: a
language-neutral ownership and lifecycle contract, small Go and Python
bindings, executable cookbooks, and a common fault-conformance profile. Its
controlling boundary remains explicit: Temporal recovers durable procedure;
the application supplies stable identity, authority, and recovery policy; and
the accepting destination must enforce or reconcile effects.

The specification intentionally excludes a hosted agent runtime, broad vendor
support claims, cross-host recovery, and a performance leaderboard. Controlled-
compute timing populations are a separate research gate and do not block the
repository-local correctness product.

## Research lines still open at this snapshot

- Run the preregistered topology pilot and publication population only on a
  qualifying controlled host. Docker on controlled compute is acceptable;
  provisioning paid cloud capacity requires separate approval.
- Test large artifact write, reference, persistence, completion, consumer
  acknowledgement, and orphan-reconciliation windows without placing artifact
  payloads in Event History.
- Compare Workflow orchestration with Standalone Activity durability and Nexus
  where the same invariant can be held fixed.
- Extend external-agent recovery to supervisor or host loss, cross-host routing,
  authority-store recovery, Codex App Server, and the optional Claude daemon
  substrate.
- Test recurring faults within one long-lived operation and build an operator
  diagnosis experiment for healthy waits, retry loops, wedges, stale owners,
  and ambiguous effects.
- Continue Workflow Stream work on reconnect, closed-Workflow retention,
  Continue-As-New, external cursor durability, and real provider output.
- Implement the first-wave Restate and DBOS adapters before adopting the v2
  authority and recovery-dynamics profiles in those systems.

See [the research questions](../research-questions.md) and

than this snapshot, is the source of truth for current task status and
dependencies.

## Evidence boundaries retained in this snapshot

- The vendor, versioning, and stream results are version-pinned, primarily
  single-host observations on one contended Linux workstation. They do not
  establish production rates or relative performance.
- The topology semantics and recovery roots remain admitted source-pinned
  historical mechanism evidence. Later executable changes require fresh
  append-only populations before renewing a current-source claim.
- The latest admitted Codex population predates additional authenticated-
  barrier hardening in the current tree; that hardening requires a fresh live
  population before it becomes evidence.
- A supervisor that survives its Worker is not evidence for supervisor-store or
  host recovery, and transcript/session/thread identity is not current-owner
  authority.
- No result establishes generic exactly-once external effects, universal vendor
  compatibility, cross-host durability, or a scalar winner between systems or
  Temporal topology shapes.
