## Research lines still open at this snapshot

- Protected vendor-session recovery beyond direct CLI retry, including vendor
  resume and application-owned start-or-attach/fenced replacement.
- Restate and DBOS adapters and later orchestration-topology comparisons.
- Workflow, Activity, and external-session compatibility across deployments.
- Consumer-visible partial streaming, durable cursors, and acknowledgement.
- Large artifact write/reference/acknowledgement windows and orphan recovery.
- Operator diagnosis of healthy waits, retries, wedges, stale executors, and
  ambiguous effects.

See [the research questions](../research-questions.md) and
[`../plans/`](../plans/) for the contracts and ordering. Beads, rather than this
snapshot, is the source of truth for current task status.

## Evidence boundaries retained in this snapshot

- The completed experiments are primarily single-host and version-pinned.
- Repeated controlled trials establish the documented observations, not a
  production failure rate.
- Temporal guarantees durable procedure and delivery at the tested boundaries;
  application identity/policy and destination acceptance remain separate
  responsibilities.
- “Exactly once” is not claimed for external effects.
