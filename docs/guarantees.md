# Guarantee ledger

This table records mechanisms and current evidence. "No" does not mean Temporal
is defective; it means the property is outside the cited Temporal guarantee.

The internal [coding-agent durability product specification](product/coding-agent-durability-v1.md)
and [portable protocol v1](../specs/coding-agent-durability/v1/README.md)
translate these admitted mechanisms into product and binding contracts. Schema
validation is structural evidence only and does not upgrade any row below.

The deterministic
[coding-agent conformance apparatus](../benchmarks/agent-durability/conformance/README.md)
checks schedule arithmetic, distinguishing controls, append-only publication,
and independent verdict recomputation. Its preserved calibration report has no
Temporal histories and therefore adds no live-recovery or product-binding
guarantee to this ledger.

Repository coverage is a code-quality gate, not experimental evidence. The
default product gate exercises topology correctness and replay on ordinary CI;
the four latency-sensitive population profiles remain opt-in, controlled-host
research. Deferring them neither weakens their registered bounds nor creates a
performance claim.
